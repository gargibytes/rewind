Chapter 03 of 04 · 1990–1999

The 90s

The Pop Culture Revolution

Satellite dishes, music videos and a newly open India. The nineties brought choice: more channels, more music, more brands and the very first taste of the internet.

01 / 05

Music

Indipop

Indipop takes the stage

Alisha Chinai’s ‘Made in India’ (1995) and Daler Mehndi’s ‘Bolo Ta Ra Ra’ (1996) made independent pop videos television hits.

Film Score

A.R. Rahman arrives

Roja (1992) introduced A.R. Rahman and gave Indian film music a new sound.

02 / 05

Cinema and Television

Cinema

Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge

DDLJ (1995) became a romantic classic and ran for over two decades at Mumbai’s Maratha Mandir.

Television

Cable TV and Shaktimaan

Satellite channels such as Zee TV (1992) multiplied choice, while Shaktimaan (1997) became a children’s favourite.

03 / 05

Fashion and Lifestyle

Wedding Wear

Wedding wear and bright knits

Films like Hum Aapke Hain Koun..! (1994) made elaborate wedding outfits the talk of the town.

Look

The DDLJ look

Raj’s leather jacket and the mustard-field romance became instantly recognisable pieces of 90s style.

04 / 05

Technology

Mobile

The first mobile call

India’s first mobile phone call was made in Kolkata in July 1995, a quiet start to a huge change.

Internet

Public internet via VSNL

VSNL opened public internet access in August 1995, bringing email and the web to a few early users.

05 / 05

Everyday Life

Economy

Liberalisation and PCO booths

After 1991, new brands and products reached Indian markets, and yellow STD/PCO booths were the neighbourhood phone line.

Sport

Sachin and the national ritual

Sachin Tendulkar’s rise made cricket matches a shared national event on television.
